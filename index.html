Where it all began

Our Hometown — Bhadravathi 🏡

Before the world knew our names, this city shaped our souls.

🌊 Jog Falls

Jog Falls

India's mightiest waterfall — just a drive from home. We grew up with this wonder.

💧 River

Bhadra River

The river that gave our city its name and its soul.

🏭 Iron City

Iron City — VISL

Bhadravathi's proudest landmark. Steel forged here since 1923.

🌿 Agumbe

Agumbe Rainforest

The lush Western Ghats — our green, misty backyard.

🏰 Fort

Kavaledurga Fort

Ancient ruins perched high in the forests, guarding history.

🐯 Wildlife

Bhadra Wildlife Sanctuary

Named after our river — home to tigers, elephants, and wonder.

"No matter where life took us, Bhadravathi never left us." ❤️
